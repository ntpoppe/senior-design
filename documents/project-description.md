# Chess Blunder Predictor

## Overview
Our senior design project will be an ML-based chess analytics tool that predicts a player's risk of making a blunder based on position complexity, time pressure, rating, game phase, and engine-derived features.

## Problem Statement
Chess players often rely on engine evaluations after a game to spot mistakes, but raw evaluations don't explain why a position became risky or help players recognize similar patterns before they happen again.

## Solution
This tool aims to address that gap with a machine learning system that predicts and explains blunder risk during chess games, rather than just detecting them after the fact. Models will be trained on labeled game data to output a live risk score with human-readable contributing factors.
